# Nanoporous Graphene Permeability

**Paper Title**: Ultimate permeation across atomically thin porous graphene  
**Authors**: K. Celebi et al.

## Summary
The study measures the water flow rate through nanoporous graphene membranes. 

## Key Findings
- Flow rate is 10x higher than commercial membranes.
- Selectivity remains high.
