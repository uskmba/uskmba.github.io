# Experimental affirmation of nanofluidic flow enhancement 

**Paper Title**: Fast mass transport through sub-2-nanometer carbon nanotubes  
**Authors**: J. K. Holt, H. G. Park et al.

## Summary
This paper discusses the ultra-fast fluid flow through a membrane incorporating 1.6-nm-wide-CNTs and the origin of such rapid permeation{% include cite.html id="holt2006fast" %} . 

## Critique
It is one of the earliest experimental verifications which demonstrates that fluid indeed flows through narrow CNTs and the speed of permeation is darn fast.
